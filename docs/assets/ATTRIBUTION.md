predicted board outline and piece boxes drawn over them. They are adaptations of
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 material and carry that licence forward:

## Renders — MIT, like the rest of the repository

`synth-*.jpg` are generated by this project's own Blender pipeline, from CC0
assets (Poly Haven HDRIs and textures) and public Lichess game dumps. They carry
no third-party restrictions.

## Board diagrams — MIT

`ex*-read.svg` and `ex*-truth.svg` are drawn with
[python-chess](https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess) (GPL-3.0 for the
library; its SVG *output* is this project's data, not a derived work of the
library's code). They encode positions, which are facts, not the photographs.
